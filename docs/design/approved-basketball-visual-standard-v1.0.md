# Approved basketball visual standard v1.0

**Approved by Jared: 17 September 2026. Binding for every new basketball illustration and every production derivative of existing artwork.** This supplements `docs/design/artwork-production-and-qa-personalisation-rule.md`; it does not alter gameplay or protected original files.

## Single canonical basketball

Use the exact visual design of the ball in Jared-approved **unbranded Loose-Ball Steal** image, generation ID `23e65758-f39f-4562-8d30-e8d82d7c18fa`: alternating navy, red and white panels separated by black seams, with no Spalding, VJBL, logos, writing or marks. This is the definitive basketball appearance for ALL illustrations in Charlotte's Basketball World, not just steal scenes. The image was approved in conversation; its presence in GitHub and a standalone extracted ball master are NOT yet verified. Do not claim the original manufacturer ball or its branding is part of the canonical artwork.

## Production gate

Before generating/editing any image containing a basketball, use the actual approved reference image where accessible and explicitly require the canonical panel layout, colours, seams and unbranded surface. Preserve correct apparent ball size, roundness, perspective, trajectory, possession and basketball mechanics. Do not introduce a generic orange basketball, altered colourway, text, badges or manufacturer/competition logos. Do not change approved characters, court, basket or composition merely to update the ball.

## QA and approval gate

Inspect every visible basketball in the full image, including background balls and reflections where identifiable. Compare to the approved reference: panel colours/layout, seams, no branding, appropriate proportions. Any mismatch is FAIL and must not be presented as production-ready or silently accepted. Record PASS/FAIL/NOT TESTED per asset and exact source/version. If the reference cannot be accessed, STOP rather than reconstructing it from prose and claiming a match. Only Jared approves any visual deviation.

## Existing artwork and integration

Audit all previously approved illustrations for ball compliance. Preserve approved originals and scene composition. Create separate, minimally edited derivatives for mismatching balls and obtain Jared's approval before integrating; do not overwrite checksum-protected originals. The already approved image with the unbranded ball remains the visual reference, not proof of GitHub storage or live integration. Apply the same ball standard to future configurable character/club variants. Verify the ball in the integrated game and visual regression evidence before release.
